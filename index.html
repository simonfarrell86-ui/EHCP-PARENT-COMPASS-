My Story

I am a parent of four children with Education, Health and Care Plans (EHCPs). Going through the SEND and EHCP process four times showed me first-hand how overwhelming, fragmented, and exhausting the paperwork, statutory deadlines, school meetings, and day-to-day battles can be when you are fighting for your children.

Cheshire East is simply my own personal example from walking this road with my family—every child, family, and Local Authority is different, so nothing here forces a default council onto your records.

I built EHCP Parent Compass so parents and carers can keep SEND guidance, timelines, school and council points of contact, letters, checklists, daily notes, and photo or video evidence organised in one calm place.

UK GDPR & ICO Local-First Transparency

Your notes, checklists, custom contacts, and photos/videos are stored locally on your device in browser storage (localStorage and IndexedDB). Review ICO UK GDPR guidance or open privacy-policy.html.

Legal Framework & Scope

Grounded in the Children and Families Act 2014 and SEND Code of Practice (0–25) for practical parent navigation—not formal legal representation. For advice, contact SENDIASS, IPSEA, or SOS!SEN.

🔄 SEN Support: Assess – Plan – Do – Review (APDR)

Assess & Plan

Schools must identify needs early, involve parents and the child, set clear targets and adjustments, and agree review dates under the SEND Code of Practice.

Do & Review

Agreed support is carried out consistently and reviewed together to evaluate impact. If needs remain significant or progress is limited, parents can request a Section 36 EHC needs assessment directly.

⚖️ Bullying, Equality Act 2010 & Education Duties

Equality Act 2010 — Reasonable Adjustments

Schools and colleges have a legal duty to take reasonable steps so disabled pupils are not placed at a substantial disadvantage.

Anti-Bullying & Safeguarding

Schools must safeguard pupils and act on bullying—including disability, SEND, or identity-related bullying—under safeguarding and behaviour policies.

Section 19 Education Act 1996

Local Authorities must arrange suitable education where a child cannot attend school due to illness, exclusion, or anxiety/EBSA.

Cheshire East Parent Story & Compass Guide

Parent of 4 EHCP Children

Navigating the Education, Health and Care Plan (EHCP) process in Cheshire East and across England can feel relentless when you are supporting children with complex needs. Having walked this road as a parent of four EHCP children, I built EHCP Parent Compass so families can calculate statutory deadlines under the Children and Families Act 2014, draft clear letters and SENCO emails, log home impact and photo evidence, and keep key contacts in one calm place.

Single-subject tabs: Every topic has its own dedicated tab—use 20-Week Clock for statutory dates, School & SENCO for APDR & email templates, Local & Grants for councils/forums/grants, Crisis & SEND for 24/7 support & conditions, Evidence Vault for photos, Daily & Masking for logs & checklists, and EHCP Letters & Law for Section 36 letters & Section F checks.

⚖️ Legal Information Disclaimer

Practical Information Tool — Not Formal Legal Advice

EHCP Parent Compass provides statutory guidance, templates, and personal record-keeping tools for parents and carers in England. It is not regulated legal advice, clinical diagnosis, or medical therapy.

  • Statutory timescales refer to England under the Children and Families Act 2014 and SEND Regulations 2014.
  • For formal advice on your child's case, contact your local SENDIASS (such as CEIAS in Cheshire East), IPSEA, or SOS!SEN.
  • In an urgent safety or mental health emergency, call 999 or NHS 111 (Option 2).

🛡️ UK GDPR, ICO & Local Data Privacy

Local-First Privacy • No Central Cloud Database

Your entries are stored locally in your browser/device storage (with optional PIN lock and AES-GCM encryption support where available), rather than being uploaded to an external server operated by this app.

⚖️ Bullying, Equality Act 2010 & Section 19 Duties

Equality Act 2010

Schools must make reasonable adjustments for disabled pupils and avoid disability-related disadvantage.

Section 19 Education Act 1996

Local Authorities must arrange suitable alternative education if a child cannot attend school due to illness, exclusion, or anxiety/EBSA.

Bullying & Safeguarding

Schools must log and address bullying promptly under safeguarding and anti-bullying duties.

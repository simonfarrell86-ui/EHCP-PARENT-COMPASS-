3. Children's Privacy and Sensitive Details

EHCP Parent Compass is intended for use by parents, carers, and adults—not as a social network or direct service for young children. Because SEND and EHCP records can involve sensitive information about a child's needs, health, or wellbeing, please take care when saving records or photos on shared devices.

Please do not upload photos or personal details of other children, school staff, or third parties without appropriate permission or consent.

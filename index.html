My Story

I am a parent of four children with Education, Health and Care Plans (EHCPs). Navigating the SEND system four times showed me first-hand how overwhelming the paperwork, deadlines, meetings, and daily emotional load can feel when you are fighting to get the right support for your children.

Cheshire East is simply my own personal example from walking this path—every child and Local Authority is unique, so nothing in this app defaults to any council. I built EHCP Parent Compass so parents and carers have one calm, private place to track deadlines, check wording, store photo and video evidence, and find the right contacts.
